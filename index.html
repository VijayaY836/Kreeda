Pieces · 2 players

Drawn at dawn, played all day

Kolams are drawn with rice flour at the doorstep every morning: dots first, then one unbroken line looping around them. Many of these games were drawn the same way, scratched into a courtyard floor or a temple step, then played with tamarind seeds, cowries or pebbles.
