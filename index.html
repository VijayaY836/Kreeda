A ladder-and-serpent race. Virtues lift you up, vices pull you down. The ancestor of Snakes and Ladders.

Ashta Chamma

అష్టా చెమ్మా

A race around a square grid, moved by throws of cowrie shells. Cross-marked squares are safe.

Cowries · 2–4 players

Pallanguzhi

பல்லாங்குழி

Sow tamarind seeds or cowries around two rows of pits and capture the most. India's own mancala.

Seeds · 2 players

Aadu Puli Aattam

ஆடு புலி ஆட்டம்

Goats and tigers. The tigers hunt, the goats try to trap them. A game of patience and traps.

Pebbles · 2 players

Chowka Bhara

ಚೌಕಾಬಾರಾ

A cross-shaped race game played with cowries. A close cousin of Pachisi and Ludo.

Cowries · 2–4 players

Drawn at dawn, played all day

Kolams are drawn with rice flour at the doorstep every morning: dots first, then one unbroken line looping around them. Many of these games were drawn the same way, scratched into a courtyard floor or a temple step, then played with tamarind seeds, cowries or pebbles.

This site keeps both traditions together: the boards, and the patterns around them.

“A board needs nothing but a floor, a few seeds and someone to play with.”
