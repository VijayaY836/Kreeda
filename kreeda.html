    How to Play

    Ashta Chamma — అష్ట చెమ్మ

    🎯 Objective

    Be the first player to bring all 4 tokens to the centre square (Home) to win.

    🏠 Board & Players

    • Played on a 5×5 board. The centre cell (2,2) is the shared Home.
    • You (terracotta) start from the bottom of the board.
    • Kreedu (teal) starts from the top of the board.
    • Each player has 4 tokens.

    🐚 Cowrie Roll

    • Four cowrie shells are thrown each turn.
    • 1 shell face-up → 1 step
    • 2 shells face-up → 2 steps
    • 3 shells face-up → 3 steps
    • 4 shells face-up → Chamma → 4 steps + extra turn
    • 0 shells face-up → Ashta → 8 steps + extra turn

    ▶ Entering the Board

    Any valid roll (1, 2, 3, 4, or 8) may be used to bring a token onto the board. No special roll is required to enter. The token enters at its side's starting square and begins moving along its fixed path.

    🔄 Moving Tokens

    • On your turn, roll the cowries and select any one token with a legal move.
    • A token moves forward by the rolled number along its assigned spiral path.
    • You cannot land on a square occupied by your own token.
    • A token must reach the centre exactly — no overshooting allowed.

    ⚔️ Capturing

    • If your token lands on a square occupied by an opponent's token, the opponent's token is captured and sent back to its starting area.
    • Capturing grants you an extra throw.
    • Tokens on safe squares (marked with ✕) cannot be captured.

    ⭐ Extra Turns

    You get an additional throw after rolling Chamma (4), rolling Ashta (8), or capturing an opponent's token.

    🏆 Winning — Two Ways!

    A. Traditional win: Be the first to bring all 4 of your tokens to the centre Home square (2,2). This is always a valid win condition.

    B. Strategic (inner-circle) win: If all 4 of your tokens have entered the inner circle and your opponent has never captured any of your tokens (so they cannot access the inner circle) and they have no possible move to capture any of your tokens — you win immediately, even without reaching (2,2).

    ⚠ Important: If your opponent has already captured at least once, the strategic win is not available to you — only bringing all 4 tokens to (2,2) will win the game. The same rule applies in reverse for Kreedu.
