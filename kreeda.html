    About this board

    Vaikunthapali descends from Moksha Patam ("the ladder to salvation") and the Jain Gyan Chauper ("game of knowledge") painted by 19th-century bazaar artists. The board is not a race track but a moral map: every square is a stage of life, ladders are virtues that lift you toward liberation, and snakes are the six inner enemies that pull you back. Square 1 is Janma (birth); square 100 is Vaikuntham, Vishnu's abode.

    Rules played here

    • Roll the die and advance. A 6 grants one extra roll — but three 6s in a row forfeit the turn.
    • Landing on a virtue climbs its ladder; landing on a vice slides down its snake.
    • You must land on 100 exactly. Overshoot and you wait for another roll.
    • Travellers may share a square — this is a pilgrimage, not a battle.
    • Solo mode races the clock; against Kreedu it is a straight race to moksha.
