The Sacrificing Mother and Son

King Vikramaditya returned to the tree, lifted Betal onto his shoulder, and continued walking through the night. To test the King once again, Betal began another tale.

Once, a king and his loyal minister visited a temple of Goddess Kali to pray for the safety of their kingdom. A divine voice declared that the minister and his only son must sacrifice themselves to save the kingdom.

The devoted son immediately sacrificed his life. Unable to bear the loss of his son, the grieving minister followed him. The king, overcome with guilt, was about to sacrifice himself as well when Goddess Kali appeared and stopped him.

Pleased by the king's devotion, the goddess granted him a boon. The king asked her to restore the minister and his son to life. She agreed.

In his excitement, the king accidentally placed their heads on the wrong bodies. Both men came back to life, but with each other's heads.

Betal then asked Vikramaditya, “Who are they now, and how should their relationships be understood?”

The King replied, “A person's identity and consciousness belong to the head. Therefore, the man with the minister's head remains the minister, while the man with the son's head remains the son.”

Satisfied with the King's answer, Betal laughed and disappeared into the night.

Teaching: When emotions create confusion, clear reasoning can help resolve even the most difficult problems.
