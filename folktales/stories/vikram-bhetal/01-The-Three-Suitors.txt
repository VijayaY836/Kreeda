King Vikramaditya was walking through a dark cremation ground with Betal’s corpse over his shoulder when the ghost began telling him a tale.

Once, a beautiful princess suddenly fell ill and died before she could be married. Three princes who loved her arrived too late, but each used his special skill to help bring her back.

The first prince, skilled in mystical arts, created a magical flying chariot and took them to the princess’s resting place. The second prince, a master physician, used magical herbs and mantras to restore her life. The third prince, a great warrior, defeated the demons and bandits guarding the royal vault.

When the princess was alive again, all three princes claimed that they deserved to marry her. Each argued that she would still be dead without his contribution.

Betal then asked Vikramaditya, “Who truly deserves her hand?”

Vikramaditya replied, “The warrior acted as her protector, while the first prince merely transported them. But the physician who restored her life took the role of a father or creator. Therefore, the other two princes may rightfully seek her hand.”

As soon as Vikramaditya spoke, Betal laughed and flew back to his tree, forcing the King to begin his journey once again.

Teaching: Actions carry different weight depending on the role, intention, and responsibility behind them.
