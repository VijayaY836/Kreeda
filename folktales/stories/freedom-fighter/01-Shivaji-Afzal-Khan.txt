Shivaji Maharaj and Afzal Khan

Once Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj needed to protect his growing kingdom from the Bijapur Sultanate. He agreed to meet their powerful general Afzal Khan for a peace talk at Pratapgad Fort.

Afzal Khan demanded Shivaji’s surrender and believed he had the upper hand. During their meeting, he suddenly attacked Shivaji with a hidden dagger. But Shivaji was protected by concealed armor and survived the attack.

Afzal Khan’s bodyguard rushed forward to kill him, but Commander Jiva Mahala quickly struck down the attacker and protected Shivaji.

At the signal, the hidden Maratha forces launched a surprise attack on Afzal Khan’s army. Without their leader, the Bijapur forces were defeated and fled.

Shivaji Maharaj thanked his commanders for their courage and quick action. His careful preparation and strategy had protected his kingdom from a powerful enemy.

Teaching: Careful preparation and clever strategy can overcome overwhelming force.
