### *Bhagat Singh’s Great Escape*

After the assassination of British police officer John P. Saunders in Lahore on December 17, 1928, Bhagat Singh needed to escape before the police could capture him.

Durgawati Devi, popularly known as Durga Bhabhi, devised a clever plan. Bhagat Singh cut his hair, shaved his beard, and dressed in a Western suit and hat so that the police would not recognize him.

Durga Bhabhi pretended to be his wife and carried her young son, Sachindranath, creating the appearance of a respectable family. Shivaram Rajguru disguised himself as their servant and carried their luggage.

The group walked through the heavily guarded Lahore railway station and boarded a first-class train. Chandrasekhar Azad travelled separately, disguised as a religious ascetic, ready to help if necessary.

The disguises worked. The group safely travelled onward, changing trains and eventually reaching Calcutta.

The daring escape became an example of courage, quick thinking, and careful planning during the Indian freedom struggle.

**Teaching: Courage combined with careful planning can help overcome difficult challenges.**
