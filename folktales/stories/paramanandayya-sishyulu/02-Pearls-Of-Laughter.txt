The Seven Naive Disciples and the Pumpkin

Once Guru Paramanandayya needed a white pumpkin for a religious ceremony. He sent his seven naive disciples to a nearby garden to buy one.

The gardener asked for one rupee. Just as one disciple was about to pay, the others suggested that they rest under a coconut tree first.

Soon, the gardener’s daughter Padma came by and asked, “Father, which of the seven will buy the pumpkin?”

Hearing this, the disciples became excited. Each one thought Padma had smiled at him. They began arguing over whom she had smiled at, showing off their hair tufts and trying to impress her. Their antics made Padma laugh even more.

The gardener, amused by the scene, said that only Guru Paramanandayya could explain the reason for his disciples’ behavior. One disciple rushed to fetch the Guru.

Paramanandayya arrived and apologized for his disciples’ naivety. Just then, a handsome local chieftain saw Padma laughing. Captivated by her beautiful smile, he asked her father for her hand in marriage.

The gardener happily agreed. He thanked Paramanandayya, saying that his disciples’ antics had made Padma laugh and brought her this good fortune.

The chieftain also rewarded the Guru, and Paramanandayya was delighted that his disciples’ naivety had once again brought an unexpected blessing.

Teaching: Sometimes, unexpected actions can lead to unexpected good fortune.
