The Handful of Grain and Coins

In the Vijayanagara Kingdom, a proud woman named Vidhyulatha claimed that no one could match her wit and intelligence. She put up a board offering 1,000 gold coins to anyone who could outsmart her.

Many scholars tried but failed. One day, a firewood seller came to her door and offered his firewood in exchange for a “handful of grain.”

Vidhyulatha agreed, but after the firewood was delivered, the seller insisted that she had misunderstood his words. He explained that he meant a single grain large enough to fill a hand. Since she could not give him exactly what he had asked for, he claimed that she had lost the challenge and demanded the 1,000 gold coins.

Vidhyulatha angrily argued with him, and the matter was taken to the provincial court. After hearing both sides, the judge agreed that the seller had outwitted her through the clever wording of his demand.

Vidhyulatha had to take down her challenge board and pay the promised reward. Curious about how a simple firewood seller had defeated her, she asked who he really was.

The man removed his disguise. He was none other than Tenali Raman, who had cleverly taught her a lesson in humility.

Teaching: Be humble about your talents and abilities.
