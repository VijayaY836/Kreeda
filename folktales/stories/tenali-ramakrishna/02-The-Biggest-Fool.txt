The Biggest Fool in the Kingdom

King Krishnadevaraya loved horses. One day, a trader showed him a magnificent Arabian horse and claimed that he had two more like it back in Arabia.

Impressed by the horse, the King paid the trader 5,000 gold coins in advance for the other two. The trader promised to return within two days, but weeks passed and he never returned.

One evening, the King saw Tenali Raman writing a list. Curious, he asked what it was. Tenali revealed that it was a list of the biggest fools in the Vijayanagara Kingdom, with the King’s name at the top.

The King was furious and demanded an explanation. Tenali replied, “You trusted a stranger and gave him 5,000 gold coins without any guarantee that he would return. That makes you the biggest fool.”

The King then asked, “But what if the trader actually comes back with the horses?”

Tenali smiled and said, “Then I shall remove your name and put his at the top. He would be the bigger fool!”

The King understood Tenali’s clever lesson.

Teaching: Never trust strangers blindly, especially when money is involved.
